The Ts9 Tube Screamer

The Tube Screamer is a classic analog overdrive pedal that has been a studio staple since the 80s. The iconic green pedal is famous for its signature midrange boost and its tube-amp like overdrive.

The Ts9 consists of three controls: the drive, the volume, and the tone. Additionally, other iterations of the tube screamer includes a fourth control for the mid-boost. The tube screamer can be used as a boost for solos, an overdriven guitar tone, and a mid-range agent for lead guitar.

S.R.V.

Stevie Ray Vaughan famously used a Ts9 as a "clean boost" to push his amps for solos.
